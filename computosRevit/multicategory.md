# Multi-Category Quantity Takeoff Schedule

## 1.- Automate the creation of a multi-category schedule

## 2.- Populate the schedule with the following parameters:
- GPC-GrupoCosto
- Family and Type
- Description
- GPC-UnidadMedicion
- GPC-Cantidad
- GPC-PrecioUnitario
- GPC-CostoItem
- Comments

## 3.- Format the schedule with the following parameters:
- GPC-GrupoCosto: Grouped and sorted alphabetically
- Family and Type: Align left
- Description: Align left
- GPC-UnidadMedicion: Align center
- GPC-Cantidad: Align right, with 2 decimal places
- GPC-PrecioUnitario: Align right, with 2 decimal places
- GPC-CostoItem: Align right, with 2 decimal places
- Comments: Align left

## 4.- Totalize the schedule with the following parameters:
- GPC-CostoItem: Totalize
